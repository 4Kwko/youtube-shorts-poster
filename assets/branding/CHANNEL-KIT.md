# SignalCut Channel Kit

## Identity

- **Channel name:** SignalCut
- **Primary handle:** `@SignalCutDaily`
- **Alternatives:** `@SignalCutClips`, `@WatchSignalCut`
- **Tagline:** Big ideas. Sharp cuts.
- **Positioning:** High-signal moments from long-form conversations, distilled into fast vertical clips.

## Channel Description

SignalCut turns long conversations into short, focused moments worth remembering. Expect sharp clips covering technology, business, creativity, science, and the ideas shaping what comes next.

New cuts published regularly. Big ideas. Sharp cuts.

Source credit appears with every video. Content is posted only when owned, licensed, or used with permission.

## Visual System

- **Electric cyan:** `#12DDF4`
- **Signal coral:** `#FF4A45`
- **Midnight:** `#071322`
- **Soft white:** `#F5F7FA`
- **Profile image:** `signalcut-profile.png`
- **Channel banner:** `signalcut-banner.png`

Use bold sans-serif captions, high contrast, and no more than two lines on screen. Keep subtitles inside the center 80% of vertical footage so platform controls do not cover them.

## Publishing Defaults

- **Title pattern:** `<Strong claim or question> #shorts`
- **Description pattern:** `<One-sentence context>\n\nSource: <creator and URL>\nUsed with permission/licence: <details>`
- **Core topics:** technology, business, creativity, science
- **Cadence:** begin with one carefully reviewed Short per day; automate only after the clipping and attribution workflow is reliable.

## Content Guardrail

Only download, edit, and republish videos you own or have explicit rights to use. Prefer original recordings, licensed partner feeds, public-domain footage, or appropriately licensed Creative Commons sources. Store the source URL and permission/licence record with every queued clip.
